Episode 142: The Long Road to Loaves

This week, Ana and Tomás talk to Ingrid Holm, who bakes for a small co-op bakery in Trondheim, about why bread takes as long as it takes. We cover wild yeast, the slow ferment, and the one thing every new baker gets wrong.

A quick recipe for the 75% loaf, as promised:

IngredientGramsBaker's %
Bread flour45090%
Whole wheat5010%
Water37575%
Starter10020%
Salt102%

Mix, rest 30 minutes, then fold four times over two hours. Shape, then into the fridge overnight at 4°C. Bake at 250°C for 20 minutes with the lid on, and 25 more without.
